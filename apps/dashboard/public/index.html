Assay: Analytics

Read-only console over the pipeline's own data. Local-only by default, no authentication: see .env.example before exposing it anywhere.

Funnel

Pools discovered → trusted-quote pools → band entrants → ever-eligible tokens → ever-alerted tokens per level. Stages come from different tables and grains, so this is a narrative funnel, not a strict subset chain.

Launch cadence (30d)

Pools discovered per day over the trailing 30 days, with the trusted-quote subset. Bucketed by discovery time, not on-chain creation time; a backfill shows up as a spike.

Score → realized outcome

Share of the full band-entrant population reaching ≥2x/≥5x/≥10x by 72h, at or above each score floor. Score is "as known": the latest score at most 15 minutes after band entry. Population-wide, not alert-conditioned; n is annotated per floor.

Entry-feature quartiles

Median realized 72h multiple by quartile of one entry-time feature, over the full band-entrant population. Rows with a missing or non-numeric value are excluded from every bucket, not silently dropped from the count.

Survival by horizon

SURVIVED/DIED label counts per horizon. Labels only exist once observed history reaches the horizon, so recent pools are absent; this lags reality by up to the longest horizon.

Recent alerts

Most recent alerts, newest first, with their realized 72h outcome where the labeling window has elapsed. Token name/symbol are untrusted on-chain metadata, rendered as plain text only.

Judgment quality

LLM judgment-brief status mix, LIVE mode only (REPLAY reruns are excluded to avoid double-counting). Per prompt version and per week, so drift after a prompt change is visible.
